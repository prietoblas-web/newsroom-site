The Liberation Fonts are metric-compatible replacements for Arial (Sans),
Times New Roman (Serif), and Courier New (Mono). Only the Mono and Serif
regular faces are bundled here; they are used by the `daily_fold` PDF renderer
so output is byte-deterministic and does not depend on host-installed fonts
(the core PDF fonts Courier/Times drop curly quotes and other glyphs present in
ASX-sourced text).
